NetAigis OTP adds a second layer of security for your online accounts.
It generates one-time passwords (OTP) on your device that you use together with your normal password.
Codes work offline, including in airplane mode.

Add tokens by scanning a QR code or by entering the configuration manually.
NetAigis OTP implements open standards TOTP (RFC 6238) and HOTP (RFC 4226), so it works with Google, GitHub, Nextcloud, Forgejo and many other services.

<b>Features:</b>
<ul>
<li>Scan QR codes with CameraX</li>
<li>Export and import tokens (JSON / key URI) via the Storage Access Framework</li>
<li>Material design with dark theme support</li>
<li>Search tokens</li>
<li>Token details and editing</li>
<li>Optional biometric / PIN lock to open the app</li>
<li>Offline issuer icons for many well-known services</li>
</ul>

NetAigis OTP is based on a FreeOTP+ fork and maintained by <a href="https://net-aigis.de/">NetAigis</a>.
